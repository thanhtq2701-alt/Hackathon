DOC VA XU LY TAI LIEU NAY THE NAO
======================================================================

- Moi loi ben duoi la mot chu trinh rieng: hien tuong -> tai hien -> sua ->
  kiem tra -> dieu kien dong loi. Lam va xac nhan xong chu trinh nay moi
  chuyen sang chu trinh tiep theo.
- Khong sua nhieu loi trong mot commit neu viec do lam kho biet thay doi nao
  lam loi hoac lam loi bien mat.
- Luu ket qua truoc/sau, commit va bang chung test cho tung chu trinh.
- Khong deploy len website cong khai de thu mot loi neu chua co dong y.
- Cac duong dan trong file nay tinh tu thu muc goc repository.
- "Da xac nhan trong ma" nghia la co the thay hanh vi trong source hien tai.
  "Da tai hien truoc day" la bang chung cu, can chay lai tren ban hien tai
  truoc khi ket luan da het hay van con.
- Cac lua chon ve luat chua duoc Thanh/group Auraa chot phai duoc hoi/xac
  nhan truoc khi thay doi gameplay.

UU TIEN DE XUAT

P1 - Sua truoc playtest tiep: loi dang co the lam sai trang thai, sai ket
     qua hoac sai so lieu ma nguoi choi dung de quyet dinh.

     noi dung mau thuan, tinh nang mock de nguoi choi hieu nham.
P3 - Can chot thiet ke: hanh vi da ro trong code nhung chua ro co trai voi
     luat mong muon hay khong.

CHU TRINH 01 — LOI GIAI THICH FALLBACK PHAI LA HAM DOC-ONLY
======================================================================

Uu tien: P3 | Tinh trang: BUG TIEM AN TRONG NHANH DU PHONG; CHUA TIM THAY
LUA CHON CONTENT HIEN TAI KICH HOAT NHANH NAY

Hien tuong:
Neu lua chon khong co truong outcome, getChoiceOutcomeText() duoc goi de tao
cau giai thich. Nhanh fallback hien sua gs.bars[k] voi ten rut gon
approval/tension/eco/unrest/party, trong khi state that dung
publicApproval/internationalTension/economicState/stateAuthority/partyLoyalty.
Ham con cong energy truoc khi applyDeltas() chay, nen neu nhanh nay duoc goi
thi Energy bi tinh them lan nua. positives/negatives hien cung khong duoc cap
nhat trong vong lap nen text fallback khong mo ta delta.

Gioi han bang chung:
Kiem tra content cards va story arcs tai commit nay khong tim thay lua chon
nao thieu truong outcome; hai gift card dong trong app/client.js cung gan
outcome cho cac lua chon. Vi vay day la loi trong nhanh du phong, chua co bang
chung rang nguoi choi co the kich hoat no voi content hien tai. Giu muc uu
tien thap hon cac loi dang xay ra; khong bao cao no nhu loi da tai hien.

Vi tri doi chieu:
- app/client.js, getChoiceOutcomeText(), khoang dong 340-365.
- Ham duoc goi truoc applyDeltas() trong luong xac nhan lua chon.

Chu trinh:
1. Tai hien:
   - Goi getChoiceOutcomeText() voi mot lua chon tong hop khong co outcome,
     co energy khac 0 va it nhat mot delta stat.
   - Ghi lai JSON gs.bars va gs.energy truoc khi xac nhan.
   - Goi getChoiceOutcomeText() va ghi lai state ngay sau ham, truoc
     applyDeltas().
   - Xac nhan rang viec chi render noi dung khong lam thay doi state. Neu
     khong co content game kich hoat nhanh nay thi ghi ro day la test don vi,
     khong phai loi gameplay da tai hien.
2. Sua:
   - Bien getChoiceOutcomeText() thanh ham doc-only: chi nhan du lieu va
     tra ve text, khong sua gs.
   - Dung cung mot ham tinh Resolution/hau qua cho UI va game core; khong tu
     tinh delta trong ham render.
   - Neu lua chon thieu outcome, tao mo ta tu Resolution that. Cap nhat dung
     ten stat va khong cong energy o buoc hien thi.
3. Kiem tra:
   - Them regression test goi ham tao mo ta nhieu lan va xac nhan state
     khong doi.
   - Xac nhan energy va tung stat chi doi mot lan sau khi chot lua chon.
   - Kiem tra lua chon co outcome va khong co outcome.
4. Dong loi khi:
   - Ham render khong co side effect.
   - Content validation bao dam moi lua chon co outcome hoac co fallback da
     duoc test.
   - Moi hau qua chi duoc ap dung mot lan va test bao phu ca state truoc/sau.

NHUNG VAN DE CAN THEO DOI, KHONG TU DONG GOI LA BUG
======================================================================

1. Multiplayer/server-authoritative:
   Repository hien la web app chay tren browser, khong co server game, phong
   online, xac thuc nguoi choi, dong bo multiplayer hay luu run qua restart.
   Day la tinh nang chua xay, khong phai loi cua tinh nang dang hoat dong.
   Neu muc tieu ban dau can choi nhieu nguoi online, can lap chu trinh thiet
   ke va xay rieng sau khi chot product requirements.

2. Randomness:
   Game dung Math.random() cho thu tu topic, gift va khoa lua chon. Game core
   co tham so random inject duoc cho topic queue, nhung chua co seed/replay
   xuyen suot run. Neu can tai hien loi va cong bang multiplayer, lap chu
   trinh rieng ve seed va luu event; chua nen doi neu chi can prototype.

3. Khoa 2 trong 4 lua chon:
   Ma hien tai co the vo hieu pointer events cho 2 lua chon khi Loyalty thap,
   con 2 lua chon khac van bam duoc. Loi "tat ca lua chon bi khoa" thuoc ban
   local khac theo tai lieu cu, chua xac nhan tren ban hien tai. Neu muon giu
   co che nay, can test xa hoi/keyboard va dam bao nguoi choi biet ly do; day
   co the la quyet dinh gameplay thay vi loi he thong.

4. Can bang game va chat luong noi dung:
   Chua co du lieu playtest nhom de ket luan game can bang hay de hieu. Can
   thu thap quan sat co dong y; khong the ket luan chi tu viec JavaScript
   khong bao loi.
